# Start here

[Course home](README.md) · [Syllabus](SYLLABUS.md)

## 1. Choose your learning pathway

**Core (no-code):** read the conceptual lessons, predict outcomes, explore supplied visual models, traces and spreadsheets, and build a thinking portfolio. **Technical extension:** optionally use simulation, notebooks and recorded robotics datasets. The two routes assess the same reasoning competencies. Neither requires actual robot hardware.

## 2. Prepare for the course

Use a browser, a Markdown or text editor and a spreadsheet tool. Optional simulation tools and their tested requirements will be provided with relevant labs. Do not install expensive software or purchase equipment for this shell.

Read the [reasoning loop](framework/reasoning-loop.md), [thinking studio method](framework/thinking-studio.md), [assessment framework](framework/assessment-rubric.md) and [AI-use guidance](SYLLABUS.md).

## 3. Establish a baseline and select a project

Before studying, describe how you would evaluate a machine advertised as able to perform a familiar physical job. Record what you think it knows, what it can do, what risks and alternatives exist, and what evidence would change your decision. This is a draft diagnostic prompt; a matched end-of-course assessment will be developed later.

By Module 02, choose a real or supplied physical-work problem (or an enabling technology) for your [capstone](capstone/README.md). If using a real workplace, get permission; do not collect sensitive information or interfere with its operation. A supplied fictional case is an acceptable alternative.

## 4. Follow the stages

Begin with [Stage I — Think ABOUT Physical AI](course/01-think-about-physical-ai/README.md). Continue in order to [Stage II](course/02-think-with-physical-ai/README.md) and [Stage III](course/03-think-in-physical-ai/README.md). In each module: read the outline, work through the lesson and lab when available, complete the assignment, self-assess, and record changes in your thinking journal.

## 5. Keep track of publication

Check the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md). During v0.1.0, the syllabus and outline are available but the substantive lesson, laboratory and assessment content is planned, not yet ready for study. Save the diagnostic and project idea until the relevant lessons are published.
