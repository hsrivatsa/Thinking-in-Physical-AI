## Intended procedure

1. Predict results for an empty, full, fragile or obstructed cup before viewing the evidence.
2. Vary surface friction and object properties in the planned model; record intended versus observed effect.
3. Compare two possible actions, explain discrepancies and revise the prediction.

Follow the [Thinking Studio sequence](../../../framework/thinking-studio.md): encounter, predict, understand, experiment, challenge, explain, decide, reflect, transfer. Record a prediction before examining the results.
