**Status:** outline available; lesson, lab, assignment and assessment in development.

## Central question

How do perception, knowledge, planning, feedback and action work together?

## Signature mental model

The world, observations, estimated state and physical outcomes are different things; interfaces and feedback determine system behavior.

## Thinking practice

Diagnose a system failure across layers, quantify simple constraints and separate observation from inference.

## Observable learning outcomes

1. Draw a layered physical system and locate the interfaces between sensing, estimation, planning, control and actuation.
2. Distinguish reality, observations, beliefs and uncertainty; propose information-seeking actions.
3. Explain how delayed feedback, calibration and resource limits can produce failures despite a correct high-level plan.

## Curriculum outline

### Core — required concepts

- World state, observations, state estimation, partial observability and uncertainty
- Sensors and fusion, localization, calibration and degraded sensing
- Planning, policy, control, actuation and independent protective functions
- Feedback, latency, timing, disturbances and physical interaction
- Edge/cloud boundaries, telemetry, integration, people and service

### Applied — investigation and judgment

- Diagnose a correctly identified tote that is placed incorrectly
- Compare measured state with estimated state under occlusion
- Explore a simple feedback system under delay and disturbance

### Extension — optional technical or advanced depth

- Discrete feedback models, control stability and timing budgets
- ROS 2 concepts; sensor synchronization; hardware-software co-design
