## Mental model

The world, observations, estimated state and physical outcomes are different things; interfaces and feedback determine system behavior.

## Learning objectives

- Draw a layered physical system and locate the interfaces between sensing, estimation, planning, control and actuation.
- Distinguish reality, observations, beliefs and uncertainty; propose information-seeking actions.
- Explain how delayed feedback, calibration and resource limits can produce failures despite a correct high-level plan.
