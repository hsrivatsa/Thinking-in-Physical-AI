## Deliverable: Embodiment and Environment Comparison

Apply this module's mental model and thinking practice to your chosen capstone context or a supplied case.

- State the physical purpose, relevant system boundary and initial assumptions.
- Use the module's lesson and lab evidence when available; distinguish observations from inference and proposed actions.
- Compare at least one credible alternative; identify uncertainty, human responsibilities and possible consequences.
- Record how evidence changed your explanation or decision; say what would change it again.
- Complete the transfer prompt: Compare alternative ways to harvest fruit or assist a person with mobility.

## Proposed artifact-specific requirement

Feasibility table, annotated geometry, assumptions and a reasoned recommendation.

## Proposed review criterion

Can explain why a simpler body or environment change may outperform a more flexible machine.
