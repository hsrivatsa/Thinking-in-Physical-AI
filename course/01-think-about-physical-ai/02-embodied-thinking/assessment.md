## Competencies to assess

1. Relate reach, payload, precision, mobility, grip, power and duty cycle to physical tasks.
2. Compare fixed, wheeled, legged and task-specific embodiments using explicit requirements.
3. Justify an embodiment or environmental change with trade-offs rather than novelty.
