## Central question

How do we establish that an intelligent physical system works?

## Signature mental model

A demonstration establishes possibility, not dependability; the denominator and conditions define the claim.

## Thinking practice

Define acceptance criteria, test representative variation, disclose assistance and analyze evidence limitations.

## Observable learning outcomes

1. Distinguish capability, skill, task, workflow, endurance, recovery and replication evidence.
2. Calculate and interpret eligibility, autonomous completion, accepted work and human assistance metrics.
3. Design a representative experiment with baseline, held-out cases, failure dispositions and uncertainty.

## Curriculum outline

### Core — required concepts

- Capability demonstration versus deployment evidence
- Experimental hypotheses, baselines, test conditions, confounding factors and uncertainty
- Evaluation layers from components to integrated workflows
- Eligibility, attempts, retries, autonomous/assisted completions and total demand
- Evidence ladder: simulation, controlled tests, authorized pilot, endurance and replication

### Applied — investigation and judgment

- Audit a demo and a reported success rate
- Use scenario and task logs to compare headline metrics and total work coverage
- Construct a test plan, evidence register and decision threshold

### Extension — optional technical or advanced depth

- Confidence intervals and sample-size intuition; distribution and tail analysis
- Dataset provenance, benchmarking, evaluation harnesses and regression tests
