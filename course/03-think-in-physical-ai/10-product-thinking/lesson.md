## Mental model

The complete operated system is the unit of product value; minimize the breadth of the promise, not essential completeness.

## Learning objectives

- Specify a bounded product promise, supported conditions, exclusions, human/service responsibilities and acceptance criteria.
- Distinguish a capability demonstration, a minimum concept, a valuable product and a repeatable offering.
- Apply Done, Dependable, Desired and Duplicated (4D) criteria while treating safety and authorization separately.
