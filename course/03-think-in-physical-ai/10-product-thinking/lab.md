## Intended procedure

1. Identify the claim the demo actually supports.
2. Compare alternative offerings and construct a narrow initial product promise.
3. Apply a 4D evidence review, flag independent assurance gates and recommend a next experiment.

Follow the [Thinking Studio sequence](../../../framework/thinking-studio.md): encounter, predict, understand, experiment, challenge, explain, decide, reflect, transfer. Record a prediction before examining the results.
