## Stage learning checkpoint (planned)

Evaluate evidence and assurance, assess complete system value and defend a lifecycle decision in an unfamiliar context.

Use a different application from the one used in the studios to demonstrate transfer. Provide a short explanation, relevant evidence, unresolved assumptions, human responsibilities and one decision you can defend. The scored checkpoint and worked examples will be developed with the assessment materials.
