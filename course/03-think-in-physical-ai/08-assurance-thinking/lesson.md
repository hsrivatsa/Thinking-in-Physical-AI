## Mental model

Risk reduction and authorization are independent gates; performance cannot substitute for assurance.

## Learning objectives

- Identify hazards in normal operation and non-routine activities, including security-related failure paths.
- Distinguish candidate risk controls, verification evidence and authorization decisions.
- Explain why product category, jurisdiction, configuration and lifecycle changes matter for assurance.
