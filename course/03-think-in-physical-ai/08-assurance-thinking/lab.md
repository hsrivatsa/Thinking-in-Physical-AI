## Intended procedure

1. Identify possible harm and people exposed during operation, maintenance and recovery.
2. Map candidate controls and evidence needed to establish their effectiveness.
3. Introduce a model or configuration change and identify reassessment requirements.

Follow the [Thinking Studio sequence](../../../framework/thinking-studio.md): encounter, predict, understand, experiment, challenge, explain, decide, reflect, transfer. Record a prediction before examining the results.
