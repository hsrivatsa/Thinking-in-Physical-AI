## Mental model

Capability does not confer authority; autonomy is an allocation of responsibility.

## Learning objectives

- Separate autonomy over job selection, planning, execution, verification, authorization, exceptions and recovery.
- Specify an operating envelope and responsibility map for a shared physical workflow.
- Design legible interaction and human intervention with clear triggers and account for assistance burden.
