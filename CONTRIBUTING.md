# Contributing and editorial standards

[Course home](README.md) · [Authoring guide](templates/module-authoring-guide.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

This is a developing independent course. Suggestions for corrections, additional cases and teaching improvements are welcome once the maintainer opens the contribution channels. Licensing and contribution terms are not yet selected; do not submit material you do not have the right to share.

## Suggested workflow

Use GitHub Issues to propose a change with module ID, rationale, relevant sources and affected files. For larger contributions, discuss scope and rights with the maintainer before opening a pull request. Keep changes focused; avoid publishing confidential or personal data. The maintainer should review and merge accepted changes.

## Review expectations

- Preserve the three stages and the distinction between subject knowledge and thinking competency.
- Clearly label planned content, assumptions, synthetic examples and measured evidence.
- Prefer source-backed technical claims, explicitly stated denominators and representative test conditions.
- Keep required safety and authority evidence separate from commercial or performance scores.
- Provide accessible no-code alternatives and reproducible procedures for experiments.
- Check source rights, attribution, accessibility and internal navigation.
- Disclose material AI assistance and verify outputs independently.

Refer to [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) before reusing or contributing substantial material.
