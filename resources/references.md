## Course design inspiration

- Stanford University CS193T, [Thinking with AI](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs193t/cs193t.1272/), Autumn 2026, Cynthia Bailey (instructor). Its emphasis on hands-on tutorials, personal applications, comparison and reflective judgment informs the teaching approach. This course is independent; no affiliation, endorsement or reuse permission is implied.
- Physical AI Primer for Product Builders — author’s draft proposal and associated Physical AI product-thinking notes (companion foundation; not reproduced or included here). The course uses the author's concepts as inspiration and develops an independent learning structure.
