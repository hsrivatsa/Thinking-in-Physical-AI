# The Thinking in Physical AI framework

[Course home](../README.md)

The course treats Thinking in Physical AI as a proposed interdisciplinary reasoning methodology, not an already established academic discipline. Its unit of reasoning is intelligence operating within physical reality, human purpose and consequences.

- [Human reasoning loop](reasoning-loop.md): questions a learner uses to investigate a physical system and decide what to do.
- [Physical AI Thinking Studio](thinking-studio.md): method for learning through prediction, experimentation, explanation, reflection and transfer.
- [Proficiency rubric](assessment-rubric.md): proposed standards for applied reasoning and evidence integrity.

The machine's closed-loop operation and the learner's reasoning cycle are distinct. Both are useful throughout all three learning stages.
