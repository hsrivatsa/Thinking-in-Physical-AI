# Thinking journal — reusable template

[Portfolio](README.md)

Copy this entry for each studio, experiment or major capstone decision.

## Entry — [module / date / case]

- Situation and desired physical outcome:
- Initial mental model and prediction (before using AI or observing results):
- Assumptions about world, knowledge, body, feasibility and authority:
- What I investigated and why:
- Observations or evidence, source and type:
- What contradicted or confirmed my prediction:
- Revised causal explanation:
- Decision, alternatives and limits:
- What evidence would change my decision:
- Human responsibility and safety considerations:
- Transfer to a different physical context:
- AI assistance used and how I checked it:

Do not invent observations. Mark incomplete entries clearly.
